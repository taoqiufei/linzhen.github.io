Protect The Core!守护核心ARENA DEFENCE PROTOTYPE

在遗迹竞技场中坚守魔法核心，迎战逐波增强的爆炸怪物。通过击败敌人获取金币与能量，再分配资源、升级属性并选择技能，撑过六波攻势。Defend a magical core in a ruined arena against escalating waves of explosive monsters. Defeat enemies for gold and energy, then allocate resources, upgrade attributes and choose how to use three spells to survive all six waves.

概览与核心循环Overview & Core Loop

战斗 / 收集 / 成长 / 守护COMBAT / LOOT / PROGRESSION / DEFENCE
设计目标 / 六波生存DESIGN GOAL / SURVIVE SIX WAVES

让每一波都考验资源选择Make every wave test resource choices

玩家守护生命值为 3000 的魔法核心。敌人击败后掉落金币和能量；玩家可在波次之间升级，并在战斗中使用三种消耗能量的技能。核心或玩家生命值归零则失败，击败第六波后获胜。Players protect a magical core with 3,000 health. Defeated enemies drop coins and energy. Players can upgrade between waves and spend energy on three combat spells. The run ends if the player or core reaches zero health; defeating Wave 6 is the win condition.

01击败敌人Defeat enemies

应对普通、重型与高速威胁。Respond to standard, tank and fast threats.

02拾取资源Collect resources

金币用于升级；能量支撑技能轮转。Coins fund upgrades; energy sustains spell use.

03投资成长Invest in growth

移动、生命、伤害、回能和施法速度逐级提升。Movement, health, damage, regeneration and cast speed scale by level.

04守住核心Defend the core

敌人数量和组合逐波增加，迫使玩家调整打法。Enemy counts and compositions grow, requiring tactical adaptation.

明确的技能分工Distinct spell roles

单体伤害、范围减速与前方群伤互补。Single-target damage, area control and frontal AoE complement one another.

递增的防守压力Escalating pressure

六波共 80 名敌人，重型远程与快速敌人改变优先级。Six waves contain 80 enemies; ranged tanks and fast enemies shift priorities.

有边界的成长经济Bounded upgrade economy

全套升级成本 40,000 金币，波次掉落总额 44,100，预留 4,100 的容错空间。All upgrades cost 40,000 coins; waves offer 44,100 in total, leaving a 4,100 buffer.

以不同节奏与收益塑造优先级Use pacing and rewards to create priorities

生命与爆炸伤害保留 ± 浮动，保留战斗变化；敌人 2 以高生命和尖刺制造持续压力，敌人 3 用速度与低生命迫使玩家快速响应。Health and explosion damage retain ± variation for combat variety. Enemy 2 combines high health with spike pressure; Enemy 3 pairs low health with speed to demand quick responses.

数值取舍Balance rationale · 四级升级共需 40,000 金币，波次名义总收益为 44,100。保留 4,100 的空间，应对未能拾取的掉落；后期提高医疗包概率，与敌人数量和伤害压力同步上升。Four upgrade levels cost 40,000 coins against 44,100 nominal wave rewards. The 4,100 margin absorbs missed drops; health-pack chances rise alongside late-wave enemy volume and damage pressure.

01 / FIREBALL

低耗让普攻节奏连贯Low cost keeps the attack loop flowing

消耗 5、基础回能 5/s：不考虑升级与其他消耗时，回能速率支持约每秒一次施放。它保持稳定输出，也为玩家保留能量管理空间。At 5 energy per cast and 5 energy/s regeneration, the base rate supports roughly one cast per second before upgrades or other drains. It provides a steady option while preserving room for resource management.

02 / FROST

把过强偏好变成清晰定位Turn an overused spell into a distinct role

试玩中玩家偏用 Thunder。降低伤害、下调消耗並加入 50% 减速，让 Frost 从伤害竞争转为控场选择，和 Fireball、Thorns 拉开差异。Players favoured Thunder in testing. Lower damage and cost, plus a 50% slow, shifted Frost from a damage competitor to a control choice distinct from Fireball and Thorns.

03 / THORNS

高消耗对应大范围压制High cost pays for broad suppression

能量消耗 60→50，保留 120 伤害与 20×1000 前方范围，维持强力清群能力，同时降低与 Frost 的资源冲突。Energy cost fell from 60 to 50 while 120 damage and a 20×1000 frontal area remained, preserving crowd-clearing power while easing competition with Frost.

玩家耐久目标Player survivability target · 最终玩家生命上限为 2500。相对敌人 400–600 的爆炸伤害，设计说明估算可承受约 4–6 次爆炸；健康升级因而直接关联闪避失误的容错。The final player health cap is 2,500. Against 400–600 explosion damage, the design notes estimate roughly 4–6 explosions; health upgrades therefore provide a buffer for missed dodges.

WEEK 06

能量难估算、技能难以持续Energy felt hard to manage

测试建议技能停止后才回能。V2 曾尝试此限制，但玩家反馈能量变得稀缺。Testers suggested regenerating energy only after a spell stopped. V2 tried the restriction, but energy became scarce.

最终：移除停用回能限制Final: remove the recovery restriction

恢复战斗中的回能，让技能选择产生节奏而非长时间断档。Restore regeneration during combat so spell choice shapes pacing without long dead periods.

WEEK 07

升级面板是否移到玩家信息区Should upgrades move into the player HUD?

试玩者建议调整升级面板位置。Testers suggested moving the upgrade panel.

最终：保留原位置Final: keep the existing placement

升级只在第一波结束后开放，放进教程同层的信息区会增加初始界面拥挤与学习负担。Upgrades unlock after Wave 1; placing them beside tutorial information would clutter the initial HUD and raise the learning burden.

WEEK 08

玩家过度偏用 Thunder，建议加入减速Thunder dominated; testers asked for a slow

两条反馈都指向技能职责重叠。Both observations pointed to overlapping spell roles.

最终：重做为 Frost 控场Final: reshape it into Frost control

保留基础机制，降低伤害和消耗，增加 50% / 3 秒减速，与另两种技能错位互补。Keep the base mechanic, lower damage and cost, and add a 50% slow for 3 seconds to distinguish it from the other spells.

WEEK 09

是否固定同类敌人生命值Should same-type enemy health be fixed?

试玩者难以估算火球击杀数，提出固定生命值。Testers found it hard to predict fireball hits to kill and suggested fixed health.

最终：保留 ± 浮动Final: keep the ± variation

设计记录认为浮动会带来战斗变化，因此不移除。The design notes retain variation to preserve combat variety.

WEEK 09

E3 速度不够突出Enemy 3 did not feel fast enough

低生命、小碰撞体积、高速冲核是既定职责，但试玩辨识度不足。Its intended role was a low-health, small-hitbox core rusher, but the speed did not read clearly enough.

最终：速度调至 200Final: raise speed to 200

V2 的 150 提高到 200，强化威胁辨识与反应压力。Raise speed from 150 in V2 to 200 to sharpen threat recognition and reaction pressure.

WEEK 10

移动速度和生命升级显得无用Movement and health upgrades felt useless

玩家认为这两项成长没有充分影响战斗。Players felt these upgrades did not meaningfully affect combat.

最终：让敌人机制验证其价值Final: make enemy mechanics test their value

为 E2 加入尖刺攻击，让移动速度帮助躲避、生命升级增加失误容错；并随波次增加敌人数量。Add spikes to E2 so movement helps dodge and health increases error tolerance; enemy counts also rise by wave.

迭代结论Iteration outcome · 数值不是孤立微调：玩家偏好、能量压力、敌人可辨识度和升级效用互相影响。最终版通过技能分工、E2 尖刺与 E3 提速，把试玩观察转化为可读的战斗决策。Values were not tuned in isolation: player preference, energy pressure, enemy readability and upgrade usefulness interacted. The final build translated these observations into clearer combat decisions through spell roles, E2 spikes and a faster E3.
