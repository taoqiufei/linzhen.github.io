01

概览与叙事Overview & Narrative

海洋污染 / 回到陆地OCEAN POLLUTION / JOURNEY TO LAND
BAG IT ALL / GAME DESIGN袋我上岸 / 游戏设计

玩家扮演一只被人类丢弃在海里的塑料袋。它意识到海洋并非自己的归处，于是沿着海底向上探索，寻找回到陆地的路。清理海洋生物身上的垃圾让它逐渐成长，也帮助生物恢复行动。The player is a plastic bag discarded in the ocean. Realising the sea is not its home, it explores upward through the seabed in search of a way back to land. Clearing rubbish from marine life helps the bag grow and allows the creatures to move again.

故事推进NARRATIVE ARC

清理、成长、回岸Clean, Grow, Return

从海底垃圾堆醒来后，塑料袋逐步学习移动与清理。被帮助的鱼会恢复活力，并在部分区域协助打开通路。旅程最终抵达岸边，让角色的归途回应海洋污染这一主题。After waking among seabed rubbish, the bag learns to move and clean. Rescued fish recover, and some help open routes through the level. The journey ends at the shore, connecting the character's return with the wider theme of ocean pollution.

叙事与玩法相连STORY THROUGH PLAY

帮助海洋生物，也推动前进Helping Creatures Moves the Journey Forward

清理不是独立于故事之外的任务：玩家的行动会改变生物状态、开启区域通道，并让角色继续向上探索。Cleaning is part of the story rather than a separate task. The player's actions change creature states, open passages and move the upward journey forward.

概念迭代CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

早期方案借鉴拼图消除：塑料袋吞入垃圾后改变形状，消除与其形态匹配的海洋生物。经过设计与美术团队讨论和目标体验调整后，我协助团队转向“清理垃圾、帮助生物、吞噬成长”，让玩法、美术可行性与环保叙事更一致。An early concept borrowed from shape-matching puzzle games: the bag changed form after swallowing rubbish and eliminated marine creatures with matching shapes. After design-art discussions and a review of the intended player experience, I helped move the team toward cleaning rubbish, helping sea life and growing through devouring. This aligned the gameplay with art feasibility and the environmental narrative.

达到成长条件后进入新的区域，继续运用机关、避开危险，并寻找可选收集物。Meet growth requirements to move into new areas, apply mechanics, avoid hazards and look for optional collectibles.

污染成为成长资源Growth Through Pollution

垃圾既是海洋环境的问题，也是角色成长的来源。清理越多，角色越强，机制把主题直接带入操作。Pollution is both the environmental problem and the resource for growth. The more rubbish the player clears, the stronger the character becomes.

清晰进化READABLE EVOLUTION

用外形读懂成长与威胁Make Growth and Threat Legible

体积、颜色与轮廓变化提示角色进度和生物危险程度，减少只靠文字或数值判断的负担。Changes in size, colour and silhouette communicate player progress and creature threat, reducing reliance on text or numbers alone.

环境叙事ENVIRONMENTAL STORYTELLING

从深海暗处走向岸边亮处Move from Deep Sea to Shore

深海使用低饱和、受压缩的空间；越接近水面与陆地，光线和空间逐渐展开，兼顾路线辨识和情绪推进。The deep sea uses low saturation and compressed spaces. Light and space open up toward the surface and shore, supporting both wayfinding and emotional progression.

关卡设计与白盒搭建Level Design & Whiteboxing

本人绘制的俯视图 / 机制递进MY TOP-DOWN PLAN / MECHANIC PROGRESSION
关卡模块LEVEL MODULES教学 / 递进 / 挑战与收束TUTORIAL / PROGRESSION / CHALLENGE & RETURN
第一关 / 教学与探索LEVEL 1 / TUTORIAL & EXPLORATION

先教会，再组合Teach, Then Combine

起点用垃圾堆说明角色身份，并以短通道帮助玩家熟悉移动。第一条被垃圾覆盖的鱼和聚焦光线引出清理目标；之后逐步介绍上升水流、平台跳跃和区域闸门。The rubbish pile establishes the protagonist, while a short passage introduces movement. A rubbish-covered fish and focused light establish the first cleaning goal, followed by upward flow, platform jumps and area gates.

机制递进MECHANIC PROGRESSION

把熟悉机制换一种用法Reapply Familiar Mechanics

后续区域把单个喷泉扩展为连续跳跃，再加入推动玩家前进的水流和尖刺。水流从助力变成压力，推动玩家在收集与避险之间作选择。Later areas turn a single fountain into consecutive jumps, then add forward currents and spikes. The current shifts from assistance to pressure, asking players to balance collection and avoidance.

第二关 / Boss 与回岸LEVEL 2 / BOSS & RETURN

挑战后回到岸边From Challenge to Shore

第二关以强水流和海胆障碍提高压力，随后进入鲨鱼 Boss 战。终段将水流变化为潮汐，让玩家借助岩石等待时机或顶着回流前进，最后返回陆地。Level 2 raises the pressure with strong currents and sea urchins before the shark boss. The final section turns the current into a changing tide: players can wait behind rocks or push against the backflow before reaching land.

04

玩家体验与可访问性Player Experience & Accessibility

观察 / 调整 / 再测试OBSERVE / ADJUST / RETEST

01跳跃节奏Jump Timing

试玩时发现连续三座喷泉让玩家反复失败。我把数量减为两座，再调整间距与推力；首次完成后开启短路径，减少重复挑战。Playtests showed that three consecutive fountains caused repeated failures. I reduced them to two, tuned spacing and force, and added a short route after the first clear to avoid repeating the challenge.

02伤害与目标反馈Damage & Target Feedback

玩家不易察觉受伤，也会重复清理已完成的鱼。团队加入受击震屏、红色伤害提示和目标颜色区分；已清理的鱼会加速离开，反馈更直接。Players sometimes missed damage or tried to clean the same fish again. The team added screen shake, red damage feedback and target colour coding; already-cleaned fish now move away to make the result clearer.

03难度与路线节奏Difficulty & Pacing

我根据试玩反馈调整敌人的攻击距离与冷却时间，并在喷泉加入光线提示。第二关再逐步提升移动速度和吞噬范围，兼顾较大地图的移动节奏。I tuned enemy attack range and cooldown from playtest feedback, and added light guidance to the fountain. Movement speed and devour range increase in Level 2 to suit its larger map.

操作可访问性MOTOR ACCESS

降低瞄准与按键负担Reduce Aiming and Input Load

  • 冲刺使用简化输入；吞噬由自动瞄准辅助，无需精确锁定。Dash uses a simple input; auto-aim assists devouring without precise targeting.
  • 我加入单手操作选项；自动瞄准蓝图由队友实现。I added a one-handed control option; a teammate implemented the auto-aim blueprint.
认知与导航COGNITIVE ACCESS & WAYFINDING

需要时再给方向提示Offer Guidance When Needed

  • 我提出方向箭头与受阻时出现的光迹方案；目标进入视野后隐藏箭头，减少遮挡。I proposed directional arrows and light trails when players are stuck. Arrows can hide once a target is in view to limit visual clutter.
  • 喷泉加入光线提示，敌人攻击前用蓄力动作和音效预告危险。Light cues clarify the fountain interaction; enemy charge animations and sounds warn players before attacks.
多感官反馈MULTISENSORY FEEDBACK

让状态一眼可读，也能听见提示Make States Visible and Audible

  • 体积、颜色和轮廓表达成长与危险等级；可清理目标用蓝色轮廓，等级过高的目标用红色轮廓。Size, colour and silhouette communicate growth and threat. Blue outlines mark cleanable targets; red outlines mark targets above the player's level.
  • 敌人攻击前用蓄力动作和音效预告危险，配合视觉提示减少只依赖单一感官。Enemy charge animations and sounds signal attacks, complementing visual cues instead of relying on a single sense.
难度包容FORGIVING CHALLENGE

留出尝试和恢复空间Leave Room to Recover

  • 喷泉保持持续启动，保留挑战但不要求卡准时机。Fountains stay active, keeping a challenge without requiring precise timing.
  • 尖刺造成有限伤害而非立即击杀，敌人攻击频率也经试玩调低。Spikes deal limited damage rather than an instant kill, and enemy attack frequency was tuned down through playtesting.

团队功能与个人贡献分开标注；其中箭头导航为我提出的方案，自动瞄准由队友实现。Team features and individual work are distinguished. I proposed arrow guidance; a teammate implemented auto-aim.

项目协调PROJECT COORDINATION

安排周计划并派发任务Plan the Week and Assign Work

每周会议后根据成员进度与职责整理任务清单。任务延误时，我会拆分工作、调整分工或重新分派，保持开发节奏。After weekly meetings, I prepared task lists based on each member's progress and responsibilities. When work slipped, I broke tasks down, adjusted ownership or reassigned work to keep development on track.

故事与玩法讨论NARRATIVE & GAMEPLAY

协调团队收敛核心方向Align the Team Around a Core Direction

在设计与美术对早期机制意见不一时，我推动讨论并寻求反馈，协助团队把主题从伤害生物转向清理污染、帮助海洋生物与回到陆地。When design and art disagreed on the early mechanic, I facilitated discussion and sought feedback. I helped the team shift from harming creatures toward cleaning pollution, helping marine life and returning to land.

关卡制作LEVEL PRODUCTION

绘制俯视图并搭建白盒Draw the Plan and Build the Whitebox

我负责关卡设计与白盒搭建，将上升喷泉、前向水流、收集物和危险分配到不同区域，按“先引入、再变化、再组合”的顺序组织体验。I owned level design and whiteboxing, placing upward fountains, forward currents, collectibles and hazards across the zones. The sequence introduces mechanics, varies them and then combines them.

蓝图与平衡BLUEPRINTS & BALANCING

实现关卡系统并反复调试Implement and Tune Level Systems

我制作检查点、玩家死亡反馈、水流、区域闸门、镜头过渡、关卡演出、金币、地面垃圾和海胆等蓝图，并调整经验值、移动速度、镜头高度与生物体型。I built blueprints for checkpoints, death feedback, currents, area gates, camera transitions, level cinematics, coins, ground rubbish and sea urchins. I also tuned experience values, movement speed, camera height and creature sizes.

演讲与展示PITCHES & PRESENTATION

三次演讲展示Three Project Presentations

围绕项目概览、叙事与玩法、关卡进展和试玩反馈整理内容，完成三次小组演讲，呈现项目方向与迭代过程。I organized the project overview, narrative and gameplay, level progress, and playtest feedback into three group presentations, communicating the project's direction and iteration.

游戏测试与体验优化PLAYTESTING & EXPERIENCE

游戏测试并优化玩家体验Test the Game and Improve Player Experience

参与多轮游戏测试，观察玩家对跳跃节奏、敌人攻击、路线提示和目标反馈的反应，并据此调整关卡难度与机制表现，让游玩过程更清晰、顺畅。I took part in multiple playtests, observed how players responded to jump timing, enemy attacks, route cues and objective feedback, then adjusted level difficulty and mechanic feedback to make play clearer and smoother.

我也在场景交付前参与环境物件的大致布局与团队质量检查。I also helped place environmental props before art refinement and took part in team quality checks.
