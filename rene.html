05 / 跨领域游戏项目05 / CROSS-DISCIPLINARY GAME PROJECT

Rene瑞尼 · 觉醒之旅A Journey of Awakening

一段关于独立思考与自我觉醒的探索旅程。玩家通过实体机关、卡牌与故事书破解层层谜题，在发现、觉醒与重生的过程中挣脱思维束缚，重新获得自由。A journey of independent thought and self-awakening. Players solve layered puzzles through a physical mechanism, cards and a storybook, moving from discovery to awakening and rebirth as they break free from constraints.

形式FORMAT 实体解谜 · 卡牌 · 故事书Physical puzzle · Cards · Storybook
主题THEME 独立思考 · 自我实现Independent thought · Self-realisation
协作TEAM 团队设计 · 3人Collaborative design · 3 people
