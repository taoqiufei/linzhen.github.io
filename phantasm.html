Phantasm幻景 · 卡牌策略桌游Fantasy Card Strategy Game

《幻景》是一款奇幻主题的卡牌策略桌游。玩家操控公共区域中的生物卡，组合完成任务并争夺积分；角色技能与道具卡提供多种策略选择。Phantasm is a fantasy card strategy game. Players manipulate creatures in a shared play area, combine them to complete missions and score points, while character skills and item cards open up different strategies.
